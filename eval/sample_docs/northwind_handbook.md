# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook (sample document for evaluation)

## About the Company

Northwind Robotics designs warehouse automation systems. The company was founded in 2014 by Priya Raman and Tomas Keller in Pune. Its flagship product, the Atlas Picker, is an autonomous mobile robot that picks items from shelves at up to 600 picks per hour. The second product line, Harbor Dock, automates truck loading bays.

Northwind has three offices: Pune (headquarters and hardware engineering), Berlin (software and machine learning), and Austin (sales and customer success).

## Working Hours and Leave

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees may schedule their work flexibly.

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

Sick leave does not count against annual leave. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the team lead and the Head of People, currently Meera Joshi. Hardware engineers working on Atlas Picker prototypes must be on site at least 4 days per week because test rigs cannot leave the Pune lab.

Northwind provides a one-time home office stipend of 40,000 INR (or 500 EUR / 550 USD for Berlin and Austin staff).

## Expenses and Travel

Travel must be booked through the internal portal, TripDesk. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; business class is allowed for flights over 6 hours with director approval.

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to 3,000 INR per day in India, 60 EUR per day in Europe and 75 USD per day in the United States. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the trip ending. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed.

## Security

All laptops use full-disk encryption and must be locked when unattended. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and TripDesk. Security incidents must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. The security team is led by Daniel Okafor.

Customer warehouse data is classified as Confidential and must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to public AI tools.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Salary adjustments are applied once a year, effective 1 July.
